# WhexSoft Website Plan

*A Bengali-language website for WhexSoft: structure, page content and launch plan*

## 1. Project overview

**Business:** WhexSoft, a Dhaka-based software and web agency. Its Facebook page describes it as a "trusted IT partner for custom website and scalable digital solutions", with the cover line *"We Build Websites That Drive Results."*

**What the Facebook page shows us:**

- Services promoted: custom websites, e-commerce websites, high-converting landing pages, corporate and portfolio sites, clinic and doctor portals.
- Selling points repeated in posts: mobile-friendly, fast loading, SEO-friendly, secure, easy admin panel, 24/7 support, budget-friendly.
- Audience: Bangladeshi small businesses, Facebook-page sellers, startups, and professionals. All posts are written in Bengali.
- Brand look: black background with lime-green accents and the "WS" logo.
- Contact: 01335-198859 (also on WhatsApp), contact.whexsoft@gmail.com, LinkedIn page, Sonir Akhra, Jatrabari, Dhaka-1236.

**Website goals**

1. Turn Facebook visitors into enquiries (call, WhatsApp, form).
2. Build trust with a portfolio, process and clear packages.
3. Rank on Google for Bengali searches such as "ওয়েবসাইট তৈরি", "ই-কমার্স ওয়েবসাইট", "ল্যান্ডিং পেজ ডিজাইন".
4. Make the agency look professional compared with competitors.

**Language rule:** every visible word on the site is in Bengali. Only the brand name "WhexSoft" and technical terms people commonly use in English (SEO, e-commerce, landing page) stay in English, written in Bengali script where natural.

## 2. Target visitors

| Visitor | What they want | What the site must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook-page seller | Own online store, more orders | Show e-commerce service, price, demo |
| Small or local business owner | Professional look, trust | Show corporate site examples, simple process |
| Marketer or advertiser | Landing page that converts ad traffic | Show landing page service and results |
| Professional (doctor, consultant, freelancer) | Personal portfolio or booking site | Show portfolio and clinic examples |

## 3. Design direction

- **Colors:** near-black (#0B0F14), lime-green accent (#C6F432), white text, soft grey cards. Same as the Facebook brand so people recognize it.
- **Bengali fonts:** *Hind Siliguri* or *Noto Sans Bengali* for body text, *Anek Bangla* or *Tiro Bangla* for headings. Use a minimum body size of 17–18px, because Bengali conjuncts become hard to read when small.
- **Mobile first:** most Bangladeshi visitors come from phones, often through Facebook. Design the mobile layout first.
- **Speed:** compress images (WebP), keep the home page light, so it loads fast on slow mobile data.
- **Language settings:** set `lang="bn"`, and use Bengali numerals (০–৯) for prices and phone numbers where it looks natural.
- **Fixed action buttons:** floating WhatsApp and call buttons on every page (bottom right on mobile).
